About Palmer Petroleum

An energy logistics company based in Miri, Sarawak, supporting petroleum distribution and marine operations across East Malaysia.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Built for the region’s evolving energy logistics needs.

Palmer Petroleum (M) Sdn Bhd is a rapidly growing energy logistics company headquartered in Miri, Sarawak, serving the region's evolving energy demands.

The company specialises in the transportation, distribution and wholesale of petroleum products, with a strong foothold in marine fuel bunkering, ship chandelling and gaseous fuel supply.

Palmer supports clients across marine logistics, offshore support, construction, agriculture and energy infrastructure, backed by a commitment to safety, quality and rapid response.

The company employs around 115 people across its permanent and contract workforce, supporting operations across land and marine logistics.

OUR STORY

Our story so far.

  1. 2021

    Incorporated

    Palmer Petroleum incorporated in Miri, Sarawak in December 2021.

  2. 2022

    Fleet strategy

    Fleet acquisition began, starting with chemical tankers for marine fuel delivery.

  3. 2023

    Fleet expansion

    Expanded to four chemical tankers (1,180–2,000 CBM), enabling direct delivery across Sarawak's coastal and riverine regions.

  4. TODAY

    Wider operations

    Palmer operates across fuel logistics, marine support and industrial customer requirements.

LICENCES & COMPLIANCE

Licences & operational compliance.

Palmer Petroleum holds various PDA 2 & 4 licences under the Petroleum Development Act, supporting fuel transport and marine fuel supply operations across Malaysia.
