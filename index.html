WHO WE ARE

Energy logistics built around reliability and response.

Established in Miri, Sarawak in December 2021, Palmer Petroleum has grown from a modest fuel logistics provider into a marine bunkering and logistics partner of choice across many sectors. The company specialises in the transportation, distribution and wholesale of petroleum products, with a strong foothold in marine fuel bunkering, ship chandelling and gaseous fuel supply.
